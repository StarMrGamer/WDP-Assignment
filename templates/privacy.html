Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2025 | Last Updated: February 17, 2026

Introduction

At GenCon SG, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) of Singapore.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password, profile picture, and role (senior/youth).
  • User Content: Stories, community posts, messages, and event registrations.
  • System Data: Browser information, device type, and usage patterns for site optimization.
  • Badge & Streak Progress: Activity logs used for gamification and volunteer certification.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for the purpose of providing and improving our services:

  • To facilitate communication between youth and seniors.
  • To personalize your experience and track your volunteer progress.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To ensure the safety and security of all users through content moderation.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share information when:

  • Explicit consent is given.
  • Required by law or to protect user safety.
  • Service providers (like cloud storage) require it to maintain our platform.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can manage your profile settings directly or contact our support team for assistance.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at privacy@genconsg.com or use our Support Portal.
