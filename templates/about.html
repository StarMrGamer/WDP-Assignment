About GenCon SG

Bridging generations through meaningful connections.

Our Mission

GenCon SG (Generational Connect Singapore) is a platform dedicated to fostering relationships between youth volunteers and seniors in our community. Our mission is to combat social isolation among seniors while providing youth with valuable opportunities for cultural exchange, storytelling, and personal growth.

What We Do

Storytelling

Preserving history and wisdom through shared stories and digital scrapbooks.

Community Events

Organizing interactive workshops, games, and cultural celebrations.

Interactive Play

Engaging in traditional and modern games to build cognitive skills and fun.

Support Network

Creating a safe space for seniors to find companionship and digital assistance.

Our Story

Developed as part of the NYP Web Development Project by Team Rai, GenCon SG was born from the observation that our aging population often lacks digital connection with the younger generation. By leveraging modern technology, we aim to make volunteering more accessible and rewarding for youth, while providing seniors with the social engagement they deserve.
